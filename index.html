Fifteen years in search, ads and recommendation. Now building real-time voice agents.

I'm Zhang Xian, an AI algorithm expert based in Beijing. I spent more than a decade on search, advertising and recommendation algorithms; since 2025 I have moved to LLM agents, working on the dialogue engine, decision models and inference performance of full-duplex voice agents.

What I work on

From ranking models to conversational agents, the question stays the same: making the right decision under real latency and cost constraints.

Real-time voice agents
A full-duplex voice dialogue engine: semantic barge-in and overlapping-speech handling, SOP-driven flow orchestration, and outbound sales and customer-service conversations at sub-second end-to-end latency.
LLM decisions and rule selection
Recasting the choice of flow node and business rule as a judgment question: a lightweight model acts as the selector, then the reply is generated. Compared with general-purpose agent frameworks, quality is on par while token usage and latency drop sharply.
Inference performance and capacity planning
Concurrency, P95 and prefill-contention analysis on H20 and H200, load balancing and capacity estimation; an understanding of the inference stack from tensor cores and online softmax to MoE communication.
Evaluation and iteration loop
An SOP-compliance evaluation system and a Langfuse-based evaluation service; online errors are turned into correction samples for the next training round.
Search, ads and recommendation
Fifteen years of ranking, CTR prediction and recommender-system experience, with a focus on problem definition, multi-objective trade-offs and the long-term health of the ecosystem.
Currently exploring
Reinforcement learning and post-training for dialogue (self-play, GRPO, DPO), and deal-closing agents that practice by conversing with themselves and keep improving.
